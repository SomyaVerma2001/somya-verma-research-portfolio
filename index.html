Selected Projects

Main research projects.

Three threads connect the portfolio: fast catalyst synthesis, probabilistic optimization, and electronic structure-informed alloy design.

01

CO2 Hydrogenation | Direct Joule Synthesis

Direct Joule Synthesized Iron-Based Catalysts for CO2 Hydrogenation to Gasoline- and Jet-Fuel-Range Hydrocarbons

A rapid, solvent-free synthesis route combining direct Joule synthesis with high-energy ball milling to prepare Fe-based catalysts for CO2 hydrogenation. The FeCoNa-B25-4-Ar catalyst reaches 57.3% CO2 conversion, 42.1% C5+ selectivity, and 24.1% C5+ yield at 350 C and 30 bar.

The work shows that initially metallic FeCo alloy precatalysts reconstruct under reaction conditions into mixed oxide-carbide interfaces, where Fe3O4 supports CO2 activation and chi-Fe5C2 drives C-C coupling.

  • Direct Joule synthesis
  • FeCoNa catalysts
  • Gasoline and jet fuels
  • C5+ hydrocarbons
02

Bayesian Optimization | Multi-Task Gaussian Processes

AI-Driven Catalyst Optimization Using Multi-Task Gaussian Processes and Bayesian Optimisation

A probabilistic optimization workflow for Fe-Co-Na-Zn Fischer-Tropsch catalyst discovery, combining weighted multi-task Gaussian Processes with argon-only yield checks to optimize C5+ yield.

The BO workflow uses 153 training rows, 42 unique training compositions, and 6020 candidate rows to select exploit and explore candidates while balancing yield, uncertainty, and experimentally practical reactor conditions across pressure, temperature, and GHSV.

  • Multi-task GP
  • Bayesian optimisation
  • C5+ yield
  • High-throughput catalysis
03

Materials Informatics | High-Entropy Alloys

Enhanced Modeling of Electronic Structure-Mechanical Property Relationships in High-Entropy Alloys Through Data Driven Analysis

A data-driven framework integrating density-of-states-derived electronic features with machine learning to predict yield strength and plasticity in high-entropy alloys more accurately than composition-only models.

PCA decomposes DOS features into energy-dependent contributions, revealing low-energy DOS links to yield strength and high-energy states that support plasticity, with Mo and V emerging as key chemistry signals.

  • High-entropy alloys
  • Density of states
  • PCA
  • Materials informatics
